SKCore BCMA

Right patient, right medicine, right dose, right route, right time — checked at the bedside.

Bedside barcode scanning of the patient and the medicine against the active order — with an electronic medication record, high-alert double-checks and clear alerts — to prevent medication errors in busy, short-staffed wards.

Key Capabilities

What SKCore BCMA Does

🪪

Positive Patient Identification

Scan the patient's wristband before every dose. Two identifiers are confirmed on screen, so the medicine reaches the right person.

💊

Medication Barcode Scan

Scan the barcode on each unit dose — manufacturer GS1 codes or pharmacy-printed labels for repackaged and compounded medicines.

✅

Five Rights Check

Every scan is checked against the active order: right patient, right medicine, right dose, right route and right time.

📋

Electronic MAR (eMAR)

Due, overdue, given, held and refused doses in one list per ward and per patient — no handwritten charts to transcribe.

🧬

High-Alert & Chemotherapy Double-Check

A second nurse scans to witness high-alert medicines such as chemotherapy, insulin and heparin. Linked to the 6-gate safety checks in SKCore COMS.

⚠️

Alerts & Documented Overrides

Clear warnings for wrong patient, wrong medicine, wrong dose or wrong time. Any override needs a reason and is logged for pharmacy review.

📶

Low-Cost Devices, Works Offline

Designed for low-cost Android phones and handheld scanners. Scans are saved on the device during network outages and synced when the connection returns.

📊

Medication Safety Reports

Scan compliance, override rates, missed doses and near-miss reports by ward and shift — ready for quality and NABH reviews.

AI Integration

AI in SKCore BCMA

AI in BCMA never gives a medicine or overrides a check — it helps nurses and pharmacists spot risks earlier. The barcode checks themselves are rule-based and always run.

  • ✓Unusual override pattern detection by ward or shift (advisory)
  • ✓Missed or delayed dose risk alerts for the nurse in charge
  • ✓Look-alike / sound-alike medicine warnings at the point of scan
  • ✓Near-miss trend summaries for the pharmacy and quality team
  • ✓Shift handover summary of pending and held doses
