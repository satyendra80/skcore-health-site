Key Capabilities

What SKCore COMS Does

💊

Protocol-Driven Chemotherapy Ordering

1,904 NCCN and NCG-aligned regimens. BSA dose calculation with weight-based adjustment. Cycle scheduling with dose modification tracking.

🔒

Six-Gate Safety Verification

DDI check, renal/hepatic adjustment, allergy screening, weight verification, cumulative dose check, and pharmacist sign-off — all mandatory before dispensing.

🤖

AI Order Safety Review

AI generates a pre-approval narrative reviewing each order for safety concerns. Advisory only — pharmacist reviews and approves before dispensing.

💉

bMAR Barcode Administration

Bedside barcode scan at infusion time. Mandatory verification before IV administration. Every administration logged with timestamp, nurse ID, and lot number.

☢️

Radiation Therapy Integration

Radiation course management with OIS bidirectional sync. Radiotherapy summary included in mCODE FHIR export.

📊

QOPI Quality Reporting

ASCO QOPI measure tracking with automated eCQM reporting. NABH digital care alignment. Network-level benchmarking dashboard.

🔬

Toxicity Monitoring

NCI CTCAE Grade toxicity recording, CumulativeDose tracking for cardiotoxic agents, cardiac monitoring thresholds, and auto-escalation alerts.

🌐

FHIR R4 mCODE Export

Full mCODE STU4 bundle — cancer condition, TNM staging, treatment plan, chemotherapy regimen, radiation course summary — for interoperability and research.

📋

End-of-Treatment Summary

AI-drafted EoTS documents from patient history, completed cycles, toxicity events, and response assessments. Clinician reviews and approves.
