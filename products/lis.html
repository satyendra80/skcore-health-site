Key Capabilities

What SKCore LIS Does

🏷️

Sample Registration & Barcoding

Bedside barcode label printing. Tube type validation. Haemolysis, lipemia, and icterus rejection rules. Chain-of-custody logging.

📋

Worklist Management

Priority-based routing. STAT flag processing with SLA timers. Batch management for high-volume panels. Technician assignment and workload balancing.

🔌

Analyser Integration

Bidirectional LIS-analyser interface via HL7 ASTM E1381. Auto-result download. Delta check and auto-repeat rules. QC chart management.

✅

Result Validation

Two-level validation — technician review then pathologist authorisation. Auto-validation rules for in-range results. Manual override with mandatory comment.

🚨

Critical Value Auto-Alerting

Configurable thresholds. Automatic SMS + in-app alert to ordering clinician. Acknowledgement required within SLA. Full audit log.

🔗

ABHA Push on Sign-Off

Automatic FHIR DiagnosticReport push to patient ABHA record on sign-off. Patient notified via SKCore PHR. Consent-controlled sharing.

📊

NABL Audit Trail

Full chain-of-custody audit for NABL accreditation — QC logs, reagent lot tracking, calibration records, and corrective action documentation.

🌐

HL7 v2 & FHIR Output

Outbound HL7 v2 ORU for EMR integration. FHIR R4 DiagnosticReport for ABDM exchange. CDSCO-compliant report format.
