Key Capabilities

What SKCore RIS Does

📋

Imaging Order Management

Radiology order receipt from COMS and EMR. Modality routing (CT, MRI, PET, X-Ray, US). Priority queueing with STAT and routine workstreams.

🖥️

DICOM Viewer Integration

Zero-footprint HTML5 DICOM viewer with MPR, 3D rendering, and measurement tools. Hanging protocol management. Prior study comparison.

🤖

AI Anomaly Detection

AI-assisted flagging for lung nodules, masses, and lesion characterisation. Nodule size tracking across studies. Radiologist confirms all flags.

📝

Structured Report Templates

Subspecialty templates — oncology staging, chest CT, brain MRI, PET-CT RECIST/PERCIST response. Voice-to-text dictation integration.

⏱️

TAT Tracking & SLA

Report turnaround tracking per modality and priority. SLA breach alerts. Bottleneck identification dashboard. Workload analytics.

🔗

FHIR ImagingStudy Export

FHIR R4 ImagingStudy and DiagnosticReport for ABDM exchange. WADO-RS endpoint for third-party viewer access.

🏥

PACS Integration

Bidirectional PACS connection via DICOM C-STORE, C-FIND, and C-MOVE. Study routing to subspecialty reading stations.

📊

Radiology Analytics

Modality utilisation, turnaround trends, abnormality rate by body region, and AI flag acceptance rate.
