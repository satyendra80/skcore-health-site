What SKCore PHR Does

🔬

Lab Results & Trends

Auto lab result pull from LIS. Longitudinal trend charts for Hgb, WBC, creatinine, HbA1c. Abnormal values highlighted with context.

💊

Medication Management

Current medication list, refill reminders, drug interaction checker, and adherence tracking. Smart dispenser integration for elder care.

📝

ePRO Symptom Tracking

Electronic Patient-Reported Outcomes — symptom diary, ESAS assessments, NCI CTCAE self-reporting, and mood journaling.

⌚

Wearable Device Sync

Galaxy Watch, Dexcom G7 CGM, Omron BP Monitor, Nonin Oximeter, smart scales. Vitals auto-logged. 6-hour glucose trend charts.

🔗

ABHA-Linked Health Records

ABDM ABHA ID integration — pull records from any ABDM-linked hospital, share with new providers, control consent for every record.

🤖

AI Health Assistant

Conversational AI for symptom triage, medication questions, and health literacy. Advisory only — recommends professional consultation for clinical decisions.

👩

Women's Health Module

Menstrual cycle tracking, pregnancy milestones, mammogram and cervical screening reminders, and menopause symptom journal.

👴

Elder Care Module

Medication compliance via smart dispenser, fall detection alerts, MoCA cognitive trend tracking, and caregiver dashboard with daily status.

👶

Child Health Module

Vaccination schedule (WHO + IAP), growth chart, developmental milestone tracker, and pediatrician visit log.
