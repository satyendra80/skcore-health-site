The problem we are working on

  • 79.9% shortfall of specialist doctors at rural Community Health Centres (Health Dynamics of India 2022-23)
  • 70–92% treatment gap for mental disorders (National Mental Health Survey 2015-16)
  • 14+ lakh new cancer cases a year, with many rural patients travelling far for each chemotherapy cycle (ICMR, via PIB)
  • 24.6% of rural women had ever used the internet (NFHS-5, 2019-21)

Our focus: records, mental health, cancer care and medication safety that work in local languages, on low-cost phones and with patchy connectivity — connected to ABDM, with human-supervised AI.
