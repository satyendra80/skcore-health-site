About SKCore

We Build the Future of Healthcare

SKCore Health Technologies is an AI-enabled healthcare technology company — delivering integrated products, digital transformation, and expert consulting for hospitals and healthcare networks across India.

Our Mission

Smart Knowledge at the Core of Healthcare

We believe AI-enabled healthcare technology should make clinicians more effective, not replace their judgment. Every product we build puts the clinician in control — with AI as the assistant, never the decision-maker.

Our mission is to give every hospital in India the digital infrastructure and AI capabilities they need to deliver safe, efficient, and data-driven care.
